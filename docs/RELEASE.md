## 4. Google Play Console Questionnaire Declarations

### Target Audience & Content Rating
- **Target Age Groups**: Ages 5 & Under, Ages 6–8, Ages 9–12, and Parents/Everyone.
- **Designed for Families Program**: **YES** (Must enroll).
- **Violence**: None (Cartoon bonk is purely playful, comic, non-injurious).
- **Sexuality / Mature Content**: None.
- **Language**: Clean, polite, and educational.
- **IARC Rating**: Everyone (ESRB) / PEGI 3 / USK 0.

### Google Play Data Safety Form
- **Does your app collect or share user data?**: **NO**.
  - No personal data collected (No name, email, phone number, or user ID).
  - No location data collected.
  - No financial or payment info collected inside the app.
  - No health or fitness data.
  - No photos, audio recordings, or files collected.
  - No advertising identifiers (AAID) accessed.
- **Data Transfer**: App is strictly offline. Zero data transmitted over the internet.
- **Data Encryption**: All local preferences and Room database tables reside exclusively in private app sandbox storage.
- **Account Deletion URL**: N/A (ArrowBuddies does not have user accounts or cloud profiles).

## 5. Monetization Hooks & Play Billing Guide

ArrowBuddies ships with all monetization hooks **DISABLED BY DEFAULT** (`isMonetizationEnabled = false` in `MonetizationManager`).

If activating optional monetization in future updates:
1. **Rewarded Ad Placements**:
   - `showRewardedRevive`: Watched voluntarily when hearts reach 0 to gain +1 revive heart.
   - `showRewardedHint`: Watched voluntarily to obtain +1 free hint.
   - *Requirement*: Only Google Play Families Self-Certified Ad Networks (e.g., Google AdMob Families) may be integrated, with COPPA flag `tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true`.
2. **Buddy Supporter Pack (Play Billing)**:
   - Product ID: `com.kasif.arrowbuddies.supporter_pack`
   - Type: One-time In-App Purchase (Non-consumable).
   - Unlocks: Golden Crown accessory for all buddies + 500 bonus berries. Purely cosmetic tip-jar support.
   - *Requirement*: No countdown purchase pressure, no pay-to-win barriers.

## 6. Release Build Instructions

### Prerequisites
Ensure `keystore.properties` is present in the project root:
```properties
storeFile=release.keystore
storePassword=arrowbuddies123
keyAlias=arrowbuddies
keyPassword=arrowbuddies123
```

### Build Signed Release Android App Bundle (AAB)
```bash
./gradlew bundleRelease
```
The output AAB bundle will be located at:
`app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`

### Build Signed Release APK (For Direct Device Testing)
```bash
./gradlew assembleRelease
```
The output APK will be located at:
`app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk`
